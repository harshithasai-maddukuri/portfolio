🧠Mental Health Detection from Reddit

Machine learning

The model that looked too good

An NLP pipeline that classifies 5,957 Reddit posts, tested across 60 configurations of TF-IDF features, SMOTE class balancing, and five classifiers.

Partway through, the scores looked suspicious. I found a label leakage bug, fixed it, and reran the whole pipeline before reporting anything. The honest result: 84.98% accuracy and 0.8371 macro F1, stable across five-fold cross-validation.
